Save the Worker!

Draw a path from the worker to the green exit. Avoid hazards, collect coins, and get the worker home safely.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Tap or click the worker, then drag a route toward the green exit.
2. Keep your path away from fire, spikes, water, and moving hazards.
3. Press TEST ROUTE to send the worker. Collect coins along the way!
BUILDER'S TIP
Safety first! A short safe route beats a risky shortcut.
Progress is saved on this device. Works with mouse and touch.
